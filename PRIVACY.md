# EmGestPro Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 7, 2026**

This policy explains what information EmGestPro ("the app") collects and how it
is used. EmGestPro is an on-device MobileGestalt editor provided for research
and educational purposes. It processes everything locally on your iPhone.

## What we collect

**Nothing by us.** We do not operate any servers, do not collect analytics, do
not use tracking cookies, and do not show ads. EmGestPro has no telemetry and
no way to contact our servers other than the Patreon API described below.

## Patreon sign-in (EmGestPro Ultra)

The app is gated behind **EmGestPro Ultra**, which is unlocked by signing in
with your Patreon account. To do this, the app uses Patreon's official OAuth
flow and asks Patreon for:

- your **full name**,
- your **email address** (only if you have granted the `identity[email]` scope),
- your **membership/tier status** on the
  [EmAppleFlagship campaign](https://www.patreon.com/cw/EmAppleFlagship).

This information is used **only** to decide whether your account is entitled to
the EmGestPro Ultra tier (or is the campaign owner's account). It is never
shared with anyone.

## Data stored on your device

After a successful sign-in, the app stores the following on your iPhone only
(no copies are sent anywhere):

- your Patreon access and refresh tokens,
- your name and tier,
- the date the session was last verified.

This data is used to keep the app unlocked for up to 7 days and to re-verify
your membership in the background. It lives in the app's local storage and is
removed when you **sign out** in the app, delete the app, or reinstall it.

Your Patreon account data is handled under
[Patreon's Privacy Policy](https://www.patreon.com/policy/privacy).

## Third-party links

The app contains a link to Patreon (for subscriptions) and our community
Discord server. Those services have their own privacy policies; we are not
responsible for how they handle your data.

## Children's privacy

EmGestPro is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this location
with a new effective date.
